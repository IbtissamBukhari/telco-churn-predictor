    About the model

    Logistic Regression trained on the IBM Telco Customer Churn dataset.

    The model is tuned for recall: it catches most customers who leave, at the cost of some false alarms. For a retention team, a missed churner usually costs more than an unnecessary discount offer.

    Built by Syed Ali Ibtissam Bukhari. Data: IBM Telco Customer Churn (7,043 customers). Runs on free hosting, so the first request after a quiet period can take up to a minute.
